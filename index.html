Communication Complexity of the LWE Function:
First Exact Bounds and the Hard Distribution Structure

First formal study of the CC of the LWE function — mathematical core of ML-KEM (FIPS 203) and ML-DSA (FIPS 204). Alice holds s ∈ ℤ_q^n and error e; Bob holds A ∈ ℤ_q^{m×n} and must compute b = As + e mod q via one-way communication.
